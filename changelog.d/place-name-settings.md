### Fewer place names on screen
- Place names now show only for places you're close to (within 5 km), looking toward, aiming at or cruising to, so
  the view stays clear. The diamond markers still show every place, so you can always find your way.
- Two new settings, "Place markers" and "Place names", each Always, Near or looked at, or Off.
- Names and markers fade in and out gently instead of popping.
