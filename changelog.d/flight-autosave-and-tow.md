### Saving in flight, and a tow home
- The game now also saves itself every 3 minutes while you fly, not only when you dock or land, without a stutter.
- New on the pause screen: "Tow me to base". Press it twice and your ship is put straight back at the last station or
  base you docked or landed at, and the game saves there. It's free for now; once there is money it will cost a small
  towing fee.
