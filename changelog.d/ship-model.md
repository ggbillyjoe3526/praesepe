### A new look for the test ship
- The grey boxes are gone: the test ship now has a simple model of its own, light grey with orange panels, a flat
  windscreen sloping down to the nose, two engine pods with glowing nozzles and short swept wings.
- A new cockpit to match: a slim, dark dashboard low across the bottom of the view, with no frame bars across the
  window. It flies, docks and lands exactly as before.
