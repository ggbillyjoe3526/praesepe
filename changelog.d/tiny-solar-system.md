### A tiny solar system and the cruise drive
- There is somewhere to go: you now start next to Halfway Station, with the planet Calder ahead. Calder has a base,
  Tollgate, on its surface; its moon Mote and an asteroid belt, The Shingle, are a short cruise away.
- Every place has a marker on the screen with its name and distance.
- Press F for the cruise drive. It charges for two seconds, then flies fast wherever you point, slowing down as you
  near something, and drops you out by itself when you arrive. F again (or X) drops out whenever you like. It never
  flies you into anything.
- The Shingle's rocks are different for every world seed, and the same every time you play the same seed. You can
  bump into the ones near you.
- The names are placeholders.
