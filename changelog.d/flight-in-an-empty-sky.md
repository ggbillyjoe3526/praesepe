### Flight in an empty sky
- You can fly. The game starts in a greybox ship, with stars and drifting space dust to show your speed and direction.
- Flight assist is on to start: W and S set your speed and the ship holds it, the thrusters cancel any slide, and the
  ship stops turning when you stop moving the mouse. Press Z to switch it off and drift freely, X for a full stop.
- A velocity marker shows where the ship is really going, and the speed and flight assist readout sits at the bottom
  of the screen.
- Esc pauses, and so does switching away from the game. F3 shows a debug overlay.
