### Start menu and choosing your ship
- The game now opens on a title screen with Continue, New game, Settings and Quit.
- New game lets you choose your ship (the Hauler for now: great for porters and miners), then starts you docked at Halfway Station.
- Continue picks up your saved game exactly where it was saved: in the station's bay, on Tollgate's pad, in orbit or out in space at the same spot, facing the same way. It never moves you to a station.
- The pause screen has Quit to title. Quitting to the title or the desktop saves first (unless you're cruising or the ship is flying itself in or out, when your last save stands).
- Starting a new game over a saved one asks first. A saved game that can't be read is never deleted.
