### Start menu and choosing your ship
- The game now opens on a title screen with Continue, New game, Settings and Quit.
- New game lets you choose your ship (the Hauler for now: great for porters and miners), then starts you docked at Halfway Station.
- Continue picks up your saved game where it was saved: back in the station's bay or on Tollgate's pad. Once the game also saves in flight, Continue puts you back out in space or in orbit at the same spot, facing the same way, never moved to a station.
- Starting a new game over a saved one asks first. A saved game that can't be read is never deleted.
- The pause screen has Quit to title.
