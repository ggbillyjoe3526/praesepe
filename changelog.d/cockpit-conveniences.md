### Cockpit conveniences
- Locked onto a place with the cruise drive, the dashboard (and the chase view's plate) now shows how long until you get there, next to the distance: "TARGET  Mote · 16 km · 18 s".
- Hold the right mouse button to look round the cockpit without steering; in the chase view it swings the camera round the ship. Let go and the view turns back to the front.
- Press R to roll upright: the ship rolls round its nose until the nearest planet or moon is below you and its horizon level. Q or E stops it.
- Press F12 to save a screenshot. It goes to the `screenshots` folder in the game's user folder, and a note in the corner says exactly where.
