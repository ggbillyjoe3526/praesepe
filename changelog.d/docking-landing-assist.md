### Docking and landing assist
- The station's bay and Tollgate's pad now shine a faint guide light along the way in, so you can line up by eye. It brightens when you're lined up.
- G now carries on from where you are and how fast you're going: the ship can slow down but never races off faster than you were flying, and it no longer climbs high when you press G low over the planet.
- Docked at Halfway Station, the ship now sits on the bay's floor instead of hovering in the middle of the bay; it settles down gently and lifts off again when you leave.
- Landing at Tollgate is now only offered close to the pad (within 600 m), not from orbit or high over the base.
- The on-screen prompts are shorter: "G  Dock", "G  Land", "O  Enter orbit", "F  Cruise".
- A red "SLOW DOWN" warning shows when you're flying at something faster than you could comfortably stop, with how many seconds until you reach it.
