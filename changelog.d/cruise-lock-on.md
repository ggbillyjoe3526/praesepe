### Cruise lock-on
- Aim at a place and press F: the cruise drive locks onto it and flies you there by itself, turning the ship to face it, flying round any planet in the way and dropping out when you arrive.
- The place you're aimed at is framed on the HUD with "F  Cruise to ..."; once locked, its marker turns blue and the dashboard shows the target and how far it is.
- Move the mouse to take the steering back. Pressing F with nothing aimed at cruises wherever the nose points, as before.
