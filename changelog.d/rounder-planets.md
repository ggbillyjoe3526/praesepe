### Planets keep their shape
- Planets no longer stretch into ovals as much when they drift toward the edge of the screen. The default field of view is now 60° (about 90° across a widescreen monitor) instead of 75°, and the slider runs from 50° to 90°.
- The cockpit and the chase camera are framed the same as before at the new view.
- If you never moved the field of view slider, you get the new default automatically; any other choice you made is kept.
