# Roadmap

What is planned, roughly in order. Nothing here is in the game yet, there are no promised dates, and plans can
change. What you can already play is on the other pages; what changed recently is in the [Patch Notes](Patch-Notes).

## Being built now

Work already under way, which should arrive soon:

- **A start menu and choosing your ship:** Continue, New game, Settings and Quit. A new game starts with choosing a
  ship, then docked at Halfway Station. Continue puts you back exactly where you saved, even out in space.
- **Cockpit conveniences:** time to arrival next to the distance while cruising to a place, hold the right mouse
  button to look round the cockpit without steering, a key to roll level near a planet, and F12 for screenshots.
- **Docking and landing help:** guide lights at the station and the pad, a ship that settles onto the floor instead of
  hovering, and a warning when you come in too fast.
- **A nav computer:** Tab turns the cockpit's middle screen into a nav computer to pick a place and how to arrive
  (dock, land or orbit), queue up steps and start the trip with Enter.

## Next: the first playable

The first complete loop of the game: take a job, fly it, get paid, pay off your ship.

- **Jobs from a contract board**, starting with two kinds: deliveries and asteroid mining. Every job shows its goal,
  its reward and a short list of steps.
- **A mining laser** on the ship for blasting asteroids.
- **Walking:** leave your seat to walk into the base, and mine on an asteroid's surface with a hand tool.
- **The ship loan:** your first ship is bought on a loan with no interest and no repossession. Pay it off at any base,
  whenever you like.
- **A shop with three or four upgrades.**
- **Loading your saved game.**

## Contracts and jobs

Every job in the finished game is one of three kinds of work:

| Kind of work | Jobs |
| --- | --- |
| Fly from A to B | Packages, cargo deliveries, taxi passengers |
| Shoot or capture | Security patrols, chasing fugitives, bounty hunting, blasting asteroids |
| Land and interact | Mining on asteroids' surfaces, science, station work |

- **Fighting is never required.** You can play the whole game without firing a weapon.
- **Chaining:** stack jobs along one route (say a delivery, a passenger and a mining stop) for a bonus.
- **Reputation:** each line of work has its own reputation, which unlocks better-paying jobs.
- **Passengers with personality:** taxi fares chat, ask for the scenic route, tip for smooth landings, and now and
  then turn out to be fugitives.
- **Hands-on science:** aim a scan, drill a sample on a timing bar, turn a valve. Waiting is skipped, never sat
  through.
- You only leave the ship when it adds something: mining, science and, if you like, walking into a base. Deliveries,
  taxi work and fighting never need it.

## The core game

- **Three starter ships**, all at the same price, each with a "Great for" hint. Any ship can take any job, and
  upgrades cover its weak spots:

  | Ship | Great for | Strengths | Trade-offs |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Hauler | Porters, miners | Large cargo hold, tough hull | Slow, less agile |
  | Interceptor | Security, taxi | Fast, agile, better weapons | Small cargo hold |
  | Surveyor | Scientists, explorers | Better scanners, longer fuel range | Lighter hull, modest cargo |

- **Upgrades** for cargo, engines, scanners, shields and the mining laser.
- **Every job type**, reputation, passengers and chaining (above).
- **Random events:** distress calls, comet flybys, eclipses, lost cargo pods, and derelicts with small puzzles.
- **An in-ship radio** with music and news that reacts to what you have done.
- **Photo mode:** pause, free camera, filters and capture.
- **The full solar system:** more planets and moons, each with its own look, weather, a base and a twist (eruptions on
  a lava world, sliding on an ice moon, fuel skimming at a gas giant), and a few places worth landing at.
- **Sound and music**, and the rest of the settings: sound volumes, key rebinding and graphics quality.

## Later

1. **Owning more:** buy more ships (outright or on a loan), a hangar to keep them in, and a station or outpost of your
   own.
2. **Automation:** helper robots for mining, surveying, cargo and repairs, then hired pilots to fly your other ships.
3. **New places:** a jump gate or route to new areas, then more solar systems.
4. **Polish:** walking round inside your ship, a codex of discoveries, and possibly a third-person view on foot.

## Still being decided

The final name of the game, its art style, multiplayer, controller support, Steam, and the hardware it will need.
