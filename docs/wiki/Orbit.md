# Orbit

You can park in orbit round the planet Calder or its moon Mote and stay there as long as you like, with the world
turning below you. It is a simple, relaxing mode: nothing ever falls out of orbit.

![In orbit round Calder, seen from the chase camera](images/orbit-chase.jpg)

## Entering orbit

1. Fly near Calder or Mote, below its highest orbit, slower than 250 m/s.
2. When the HUD shows **O  Enter orbit round Calder**, press **O**.
3. The ship swings onto a circle round the planet with a firm push, and turns so the planet fills the bottom of the
   cockpit window. Moving the mouse stops that turn and leaves the view to you.

In orbit the ship goes round at a steady speed and height, and the view turns with it, so the planet stays in place
while the ground slides past. The dashboard reads "IN ORBIT · O or thrust to leave".

If the HUD says you can't enter, it says why: too fast, cruising, or diving too steeply to level out above the ground.

## Leaving orbit

- **O** again, or any thrust key, leaves gently: the ship carries on and its nose comes up level over a second or so
  ("LEAVING ORBIT · levelling out"), then flight assist takes over and you climb slowly away.
- **X** leaves and stops; **Z** leaves and drifts with flight assist off; starting the cruise drive leaves at once.
- A hard bump knocks you out of orbit.

You can land at Tollgate straight from orbit round Calder: press **G** when the HUD offers it.

## Orbits

| Body | Orbit heights | Orbit speed | Once round |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calder | 500 m to 4 km above the surface | 90 m/s | about 9 minutes at 1.5 km up |
| Mote | 300 m to 2.5 km above the surface | 45 m/s | about 6 minutes at 1 km up |
