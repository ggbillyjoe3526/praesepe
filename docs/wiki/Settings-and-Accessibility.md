# Settings and Accessibility

Press **Esc** to pause, then **Settings**. The menu has four pages. Every change shows at once and is kept for next
time. Each page has a button that puts its own settings back to their defaults. **Esc** goes back.

## Display

| Setting | Choices | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Window mode | Fullscreen, Exclusive fullscreen, Windowed | Fullscreen |
| Display | Which monitor to use, if you have more than one | Main display |
| V-Sync | On or off | On |
| Frame-rate limit | No limit, 30, 60, 120, 144, 165 or 240 | No limit |

- **F11** or **Alt+Enter** switches between fullscreen and a window at any time. A window opens at a comfortable size
  in the middle of the screen.
- While the game is in the background it draws fewer frames, so your PC stays cooler.

## Interface

| Setting | Choices | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interface size | 90%, 100%, 110%, 125% | 100% |
| Place markers | Always, Near or looked at, Off | Always |
| Place names | Always, Near or looked at, Off | Near or looked at |

- **Interface size** makes the HUD, its text and the menus bigger or smaller. 100% already grows with your screen; go
  up for a big or high-resolution screen.
- **Near or looked at** shows a place's diamond or name when you're within 5 km of it, looking toward it, aiming at it
  or cruising to it. Markers and names fade in and out gently.
- With place markers off, the place you're aiming at still gets its corner brackets, so you can always lock on with
  the [Cruise Drive](Cruise-Drive).

## Comfort

| Setting | Choices | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field of view | 50° to 90° (measured top to bottom) | 60° |
| Camera shake | 0% to 100% | 100% |
| Head bob | On or off: your head sways as the ship pushes | On |
| Reduce motion | On or off | Off |

- **Field of view:** 60° top to bottom is about 90° across a widescreen monitor. A wider view shows more round you but
  stretches round things, like planets, near the edges of the screen.
- **Reduce motion** turns off camera shake and head bob, and steadies the chase camera (it swings half as much).

## Controls

| Setting | Choices | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mouse sensitivity | 0.25× to 3× | 1× |
| Invert mouse | On or off: mouse up points the nose down | Off |

The page also lists every key; they are on the [Controls](Controls) page too. Changing keys isn't possible yet.
