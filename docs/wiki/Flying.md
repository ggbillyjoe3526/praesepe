# Flying

Your ship flies with real momentum: it keeps moving the way it was going until its thrusters push it somewhere else.
Flight assist does that pushing for you, so the ship feels heavy but never gets away from you.

## Flight assist

Flight assist is **on** when you start. Press **Z** to switch it off or on at any time, even mid-turn.

- **On:** W and S set a speed and the ship holds it. Let go of the slide keys and the thrusters cancel any sideways
  drift. Stop moving the mouse and the ship stops turning. In a hard turn at speed the ship slides a little before it
  lines up again: that is its weight.
- **Off:** nothing is cancelled. The ship keeps its speed, direction and spin until you counter them yourself. Good for
  drifting past things sideways or flying backwards while looking at them.
- **X** is a full stop: it turns flight assist back on and brings the ship to rest.

## Reading the HUD

- **Velocity marker:** the small marker shows where the ship is really going, which isn't always where the nose
  points. When you are moving backwards, a crossed marker shows the point you are moving away from.
- **Speed and flight assist:** on the dashboard screen in the cockpit, or on a plate at the bottom of the screen in the
  chase camera. It reads, for example, "FLIGHT ASSIST ON · holding 120 m/s".
- **Place markers:** every place has a diamond marker. Its name and distance show when you're within 5 km, looking
  toward it, aiming at it or cruising to it. You can change this under Settings > Interface
  ([Settings and Accessibility](Settings-and-Accessibility#interface)).
- **Prompts:** when there is something you can do (dock, land, cruise to a place, enter orbit) a prompt says which key
  and what it does.

## Cameras

- **Cockpit** (the main view): you see out of the cockpit window. Your head sways a little as the engines push, and the
  view rumbles faintly under thrust and shakes after a bump.
- **Chase camera** (press **V**): behind and above the ship. It swings a moment behind your turns, and with flight
  assist off it leans toward where the ship is really drifting, so you can see the slide. It never goes through
  things: if something gets between it and the ship, it moves in close.

Head sway, shake and the chase camera's swing can be turned down or off under Settings > Comfort
([Settings and Accessibility](Settings-and-Accessibility#comfort)).

## Bumping into things

Nothing in the game damages your ship yet. You can bump into the station, the base and the nearby rocks of The
Shingle; you bounce off and the camera shakes.

## How the test ship flies

The numbers are on the [Ships](Ships) page: top speed 200 m/s in normal flight, 0 to top speed in about 5 seconds.
For longer trips use the [Cruise Drive](Cruise-Drive).
